Audio description

Audio description is a second sound track in which a narrator says what is happening, for blind and partially sighted people. This plug-in finds the films and series that have one and tags them, plays the described track for the people who choose it, and adds a description track you already have. Its tasks are in Scheduled Tasks, under Audio description.

Find and tag

Each film and episode with a described sound track, and its series, gets the tag Audio description. Every Jellyfin app, televisions included, can filter by a tag. The tag comes off when the track goes. It runs every night at 04:20 and after each library scan.

Play the described track

Inactive until you choose a person. For each person ticked here, when they start a title with a described track and another sound track is playing, Jellyfin asks their app to change to the described track.

Not every Jellyfin app accepts this. Jellyfin's web app and some others change the sound track when asked; an app that does not accept it plays as it is, and Jellyfin's log says so. In those apps, choose the described track in the player. Jellyfin remembers that choice for each film and series when Set audio track based on previous item is active in their Playback settings.

Add a track you have

Put a description track you already have in the folder, or upload one here. Name it as the film and its year, such as Dune (2021).mp3, or a series with its episode, such as Slow Horses S01E03.mp3. Its length is compared with the film's, and when they agree it is added next to the film as English (Audio Description), copying the sound without converting it. Nothing is ever deleted.

Leave it empty for the folder in Jellyfin's data folder. Jellyfin must be able to read it, and to write next to your films.
