Picture shrinker

Shrinks the pictures Jellyfin keeps of films, series and cast and crew (posters, backdrops, thumbnails, logos, banners, disc art, clear art and photos of people), so they take less space. Each smaller copy is checked before it replaces the original, and you choose whether each original is backed up first. Runs are set in Scheduled Tasks, under Picture shrinker.

Nothing is shrunk until you choose. Under Original pictures, choose Keep a backup copy of each original or Just shrink, with no backup, then save.

If you have just updated from version 1.1, choose again: version 1.2 asks once more. A backup you already have is kept whichever you choose.

What to shrink
Original pictures

There is no default: nothing is shrunk until you choose.

Each original is copied once, before its first shrink, and a copy already there is never replaced. If the copy fails or the backup disk is short of space, the picture is not shrunk. Restore original pictures, in Scheduled Tasks, puts them back.
Uses no extra space. Pictures shrunk from then on cannot be put back.

With no backup, pictures shrunk from now on cannot be put back. Jellyfin can download them again from the internet with Refresh metadata or Start again, but pictures you chose or uploaded yourself are lost. A backup you already have is kept until you delete it.

On by default. Every type below is shrunk. Turn it off to choose the types yourself.
Picture types

The poster of each film, series, season and collection.
The photo of each actor, director and writer.
The backgrounds behind each film, series and season, saved as backdrop.jpg, backdrop1.jpg and so on.
The wide pictures Jellyfin shows in rows, saved as landscape.jpg.
The title of a film or series as a picture.
The long, narrow pictures some series and films have.
The picture of a disc, for films and albums that have one.
Characters and titles on a see-through background.
Sizes
From 300 to 3,000, 900 by default. A poster taller than this is shrunk to this height. 900 pixels looks sharp on a television.
From 100 to 2,000, 400 by default. A photo of a cast or crew member wider than this is shrunk to this width.
From 640 to 3,840, 1,920 by default. 1,920 is sharp on a 1080p screen, and a little softer full-screen on a 4K television.
From 480 to 3,840, 1,280 by default. 1,280 is sharp in Jellyfin's rows of thumbnails.
From 300 to 2,000, 800 by default. 800 is sharp where Jellyfin shows a logo over a backdrop.
From 300 to 3,000, 1,000 by default. 1,000 is the usual width of a banner.
From 200 to 2,000, 500 by default. 500 is sharp at the size Jellyfin shows disc art.
From 300 to 2,000, 800 by default. 800 is sharp at the size Jellyfin shows clear art.
For JPEG and WebP pictures, from 50 to 100. 85 keeps them sharp; lower saves more space. PNG pictures stay lossless.
Backup folder
Used with Keep a backup copy. Leave it empty for the folder in Jellyfin's data folder. The first run needs about as much free space there as the pictures take now.
When to shrink

Shrink pictures runs every Sunday at 03:30 until you change its triggers in Scheduled Tasks: every day or every week, at the time you choose. It runs at the lowest priority, so a quiet time is best. It sends no notification: its results are on this page.

With a weekly trigger, it runs only the first time in each month. Shrink now on this page always runs.
Deactivated by default. When activated, the pictures of the types it shrinks that Jellyfin saves are shrunk once they are a few minutes old, with the same checks and backup.
Jellyfin may still be writing a picture it has just saved. 10 minutes is safe.
