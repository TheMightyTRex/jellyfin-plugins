Picture shrinker

Shrinks the posters and photos of cast and crew Jellyfin keeps, so they take less space. Each original is backed up and each smaller copy is checked before it replaces the original. Runs are set in Scheduled Tasks, under Picture shrinker.

What to shrink
The poster of each film, series, season and collection in Jellyfin's metadata folder. Backdrops, logos and other pictures are left alone.
The photo of each actor, director and writer.
Sizes
A poster taller than this is shrunk to this height, from 300 to 3,000. 900 pixels looks sharp on a television.
A photo of a cast or crew member wider than this is shrunk to this width, from 100 to 2,000.
For JPEG and WebP pictures, from 50 to 100. 85 keeps them sharp; lower saves more space. PNG pictures stay lossless.
Backup
Activated by default. Each original is copied once, before its first shrink, and a copy already there is never replaced. If the copy fails or the backup disk is short of space, the picture is not shrunk. Restore original pictures, in Scheduled Tasks, puts them back.

Backup is deactivated: pictures shrunk from now on cannot be put back. Jellyfin can download them again from the internet with Refresh metadata, but pictures you chose or uploaded yourself are lost.

Leave it empty for the folder in Jellyfin's data folder. The first run needs about as much free space there as the pictures take now.
When to shrink

Shrink pictures runs every Sunday at 03:30 until you change its triggers in Scheduled Tasks: every day or every week, at the time you choose. It runs at the lowest priority, so a quiet time is best. It sends no notification: its results are on this page.

With a weekly trigger, it runs only the first time in each month. Shrink now on this page always runs.
Deactivated by default. When activated, posters and photos Jellyfin saves are shrunk once they are a few minutes old, with the same checks and backup.
